Our Story

Fifteen Years of Beauty in Montreal

For fifteen years, Mamta Beauty Salon has been a trusted name in Montreal for hair, skin and bridal artistry. What began as a passion for helping clients look and feel their best has grown into a salon known for careful, personal service — and a 5.0 rating across 96 reviews.

Every appointment starts with listening: your hair, your skin, your occasion. From there, we tailor each cut, colour, facial or bridal look to you, so you leave feeling polished, relaxed and confident.

  • Hair studio — cuts, colour, balayage & keratin
  • Skin & facials, waxing and threading
  • Bridal hair, makeup & pre-bridal glow programs
